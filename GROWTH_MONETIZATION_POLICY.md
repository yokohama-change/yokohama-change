# YOKOHAMA CHANGE — Free Beta Growth & Monetization Policy

Updated: 2026-08-25

## Principle

YOKOHAMA CHANGE will prioritize trust, repeat usefulness, and organic audience growth before monetization.

The service will remain free during the beta growth phase. Monetization will not start on a fixed calendar date and will not be triggered by traffic alone.

Core rule: **Do not charge until users clearly receive enough value that automatic delivery is worth paying for.**

## First monetization review gate

Paid features may be reviewed only after all of the following are met. These are review gates, not an automatic launch trigger.

1. **Audience** — at least 1,000 monthly unique users once privacy-friendly site analytics are installed.
2. **Repeat use** — at least 20% returning-user rate over a representative 28–30 day period.
3. **Product use** — MY FIT is used by at least 100 unique users/devices per month or at least 10% of monthly users, whichever is more meaningful after analytics are available.
4. **Quality** — at least 30 consecutive days without a critical user-facing false-open incident; collector/API/stale-data safeguards must continue to fail closed.
5. **Paid intent** — clear evidence that users want time-saving automatic delivery, such as at least 10 explicit requests/opt-ins for notification or equivalent direct paid-intent feedback.

If traffic reaches the audience gate but repeat use, product use, quality, or paid intent is weak, the free beta continues.

## What stays free during beta

- Current open opportunities
- Commercial-score prioritization
- MY FIT preview/selection
- 48H / TODAY on-site priority display
- Expired-item auto-exclusion and quality proof
- Public JSON / CSV / RSS feeds currently exposed by the site

## Possible future paid value

Paid value should be automation and saved time, not basic access to public information. Candidate paid features include:

- MY FIT opportunities delivered automatically without visiting the site
- Deadline-stage delivery such as preparation window, 48H, and TODAY
- User-specific notification routing
- Saved profiles across devices, only if privacy and account design are ready

## Quality before revenue

- Never broaden open-opportunity rules to increase apparent inventory.
- Never mix gBizINFO historical/corporate-update signals into current procurement/subsidy opportunities.
- Hide high-priority alerts when collector/API/quality/freshness checks fail.
- Prefer zero alerts to a questionable alert.
- Final application/contract decisions always require checking the official source.

## Analytics requirement

Accurate public-site traffic analytics are not yet installed. Do not claim monthly users, return rate, or MY FIT usage until a privacy-respecting analytics mechanism is actually connected and verified.

Repository traffic is not a substitute for GitHub Pages visitor analytics.
